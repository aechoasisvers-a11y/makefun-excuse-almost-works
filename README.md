A [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5) entry: the one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week.

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Dentist moved my root canal to 9am, they charge $80 if I miss it. Can I swap shifts today?

90 characters, 19 words — inside the gate of 1–140 characters and at most 20 words.

The excuse works because it is the kind of thing a tired manager almost believes: a real, specific, boring appointment with a financial penalty for missing it, asked in a hurry with no setup and no signature.
